# MuscleMetrics Website Specification

> Status: planned redesign of the current legal-focused site into the official product website.

## Objective
The website should:
- explain what MuscleMetrics is and why it is different;
- showcase what the app does well today;
- preview the future Coach/PT platform without presenting it as live;
- tell the authentic founder/product story;
- encourage App Store download and following the product;
- establish trust through privacy and transparent product principles;
- look unmistakably connected to the app.

## Non-negotiable
Preserve the existing Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions pages/routes. The marketing redesign must not break legal URLs already used by the app/store.

## Visual system
The website should inherit the app's brand system:
- Metric Blue `#2563EB`
- Momentum Green `#22C55E`
- Peak Gold `#F59E0B`
- Controlled Red `#EF4444`
- app light/dark neutrals
- Inter typography and the same spacing/radius character where appropriate.

The website may adapt the system for responsive web, but should not create a second MuscleMetrics brand.

## Homepage information architecture
1. **Hero** — concise product promise, real app imagery, download CTA.
2. **Why MuscleMetrics** — structured logging + useful long-term analysis.
3. **What it does today** — plans, logging, history, goals, analytics, consistency/social where appropriate.
4. **Progress/analytics showcase** — real product screens and understandable examples.
5. **Built for actual training** — fast logging, recovery from interruptions, mobile-first.
6. **Founder story** — authentic reason the product exists and is being built.
7. **Coach/PT preview** — clearly labelled future direction.
8. **Trust/privacy** — transparent data/product principles and legal links.
9. **Final CTA** — App Store/download/follow.

## Copy rules
- Do not claim planned prediction/readiness or Coach functionality is live.
- Do not use generic transformation/bodybuilding claims.
- Avoid supplement-brand language, fake urgency and exaggerated AI claims.
- Prefer specific product capabilities and screenshots.
- Founder copy should sound personal rather than corporate.

## Visual rules
- Use actual app UI/screenshots as the primary visual evidence.
- Mobile-first responsive layout.
- Clean, premium, data-led presentation.
- No generic stock bodybuilding imagery as the core identity.
- App cards/charts should preserve the product's visual language.

## Current vs future
Use explicit language such as **Available now** and **Coming later / Coach preview** so visitors cannot confuse roadmap concepts with shipped features.

## Trust
Surface:
- Privacy Policy.
- Terms & Conditions.
- Clear explanation of how user data supports progress features where appropriate.
- No medical/health-performance claims unsupported by the product.

## Technical requirements
The exact website implementation stack is not established by the mobile repository and should be documented in the website repository once chosen.

Baseline requirements:
- semantic accessible HTML;
- responsive layout;
- good Core Web Vitals;
- SEO metadata/Open Graph;
- App Store CTA tracking;
- accessible contrast/focus states;
- preserve legal routes;
- avoid unnecessary client-side JavaScript.

## SEO content themes
Natural-language coverage should include workout planning, workout logging, gym progress tracking and strength/progress analytics without keyword stuffing.

## Analytics
Track meaningful conversion events rather than every interaction:
- App Store CTA;
- key section engagement if useful;
- outbound social/follow action.

Do not introduce invasive tracking merely for vanity metrics.

## Definition of done
The website is complete when a new visitor can understand:
1. what MuscleMetrics is;
2. what they can do with it today;
3. why it is different;
4. what is coming later;
5. why they can trust it;
6. how to download it.
